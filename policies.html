Policies & Community Guidelines

Participate responsibly, respectfully and safely.

Privacy Policy Terms & Rules Moderation Grievances
Important: Mulund Civic Hub is an independent community initiative. It is not an official Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) website and does not represent BMC. Reports submitted here are not automatically official government complaints.

1. Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the platform intends to handle information. It must be checked against actual website and database practices before public launch.

Information processed

Depending on how you use the service, information may include your account email, authentication information, complaint title and description, category, area, status, timestamps, community proposals, votes and moderation records. Hosting and service providers may also process technical information.

Purpose

Information may be used to manage accounts and civic reports, operate community features, prevent abuse, maintain security and respond to requests. It should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or required consent.

Public and private information

Private complaint records and account details should not be public. Approved community posts may display their title, category, area and description. Do not include phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents or sensitive personal details in public submissions.

An anonymous display name does not guarantee that the platform operator or service providers cannot identify the account behind a submission.

Providers and security

The platform uses Supabase for authentication and database services and Vercel for hosting. Access controls and suitable security measures are necessary. Review the providers' own privacy documentation for details about their services.

Retention and requests

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, security, legal obligations and dispute handling. Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their information through the platform's published contact method. Requests may require verification and may be subject to applicable legal or operational limits.

Policy updates

Material changes should be communicated appropriately. A real privacy contact must be published before the platform opens to the public.

2. Terms & Community Rules

Users are expected to follow these rules when creating accounts, submitting complaints, voting or participating in the community.

Expected conduct

  • Provide accurate information to the best of your knowledge.
  • Focus on civic issues and observable facts.
  • Be respectful and avoid personal attacks.
  • Respect others' privacy and applicable law.

Prohibited conduct

  • Knowingly false reports or fabricated evidence.
  • Threats, harassment, hate speech, discriminatory abuse or spam.
  • Publishing another person's private details without proper authority.
  • Impersonating residents, officials or organisations.
  • Unauthorised access, unlawful activity or copyright infringement.

Accounts and reports

Users should protect their account credentials. Community submissions may be reviewed before publication. Content or accounts may be restricted for rule violations, subject to review and applicable procedures.

Independent service

A report submitted here is not automatically submitted to BMC or another public authority. Use the relevant official channel when an official complaint or emergency response is required.

Service limitations

The platform cannot guarantee that user-submitted information is correct, that a public authority will respond, or that a civic issue will be resolved.

3. Moderation Policy

Moderation aims to keep community content relevant, respectful and safer for residents.

Review process

  1. A user submits a community proposal.
  2. An authorised moderator reviews relevance, privacy and rule compliance.
  3. The moderator may approve, reject, restrict or request changes.
  4. Where practical, the contributor is informed of the decision and reason.

Content restrictions

Content may be rejected or removed if it exposes personal information, contains threats or harassment, impersonates someone, constitutes spam, contains unlawful material or makes serious unsupported allegations about an identifiable person.

Voting and collective representations

Votes show community support; they do not prove an allegation is true. Reaching a vote threshold does not automatically publish content or send an email to BMC. Any collective representation must be reviewed and explicitly approved by an authorised administrator before sending.

Appeals

Users may request a review of moderation decisions through the grievance procedure below. Significant decisions should be handled consistently and documented. Urgent privacy or safety concerns may require temporary restrictions while a review takes place.

4. Grievance Redressal Procedure

What you can report

  • Privacy concerns or exposure of personal information.
  • Harassment, impersonation or misuse of the platform.
  • Moderation decisions and appeals.
  • Account access, correction or deletion requests.
  • Technical problems affecting the service.

How to raise a grievance

  1. Contact the platform operator using the verified contact method published on the website.
  2. Describe the issue and include a relevant post or reference if available. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.
  3. The operator should acknowledge the request and review it within a published, realistic timeframe.
  4. The operator may request necessary clarification, take appropriate steps to limit harmful content and communicate the outcome.
  5. If you disagree with a moderation decision, request another review and explain why it should be reconsidered.

Response times and escalation

Specific response timelines and the responsible contact person must be published before public launch. For an immediate threat or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service rather than relying on this site.
